Platform Features

Manage Electricity Issues Efficiently

The system provides dedicated functions for residents, Electricity Department Staff, Senior Electricity Officers, and administrators.

⚡

Report Electricity Issues

Residents can submit power cut, streetlight, electric pole damage, and voltage issue complaints.

📍

Provide Complaint Details

Include the complaint location, date, time, supporting image, and preferred 24 or 48-hour resolution period.

✓

Track Resolution

Residents can view complaint status, previous complaint history, notifications, and resolution information.

📊

Power Cut Predictions

View predicted power cut information for a selected area based on available complaint patterns.

🛠

Department Processing

Assigned Electricity Department Staff can accept complaints, update status, add repair notes, and mark completed work as resolved.

🔔

Automated Escalation

Unresolved complaints are automatically escalated when the selected 24 or 48-hour resolution period expires.

Complaint Process

From Complaint to Resolution

Follow the complaint through the department's processing workflow.

01

Submit

Resident submits an electricity complaint with the required details.

02

Assign

The complaint is assigned to an appropriate Electricity Department Staff member.

03

Process

Staff accepts the complaint, performs the work, and records repair information.

04

Resolve

The complaint is marked as resolved after the required work is completed.

05

Escalate

If the selected resolution period expires, the complaint is escalated to a Senior Electricity Officer.
