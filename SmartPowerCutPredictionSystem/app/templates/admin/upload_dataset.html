Prediction Workflow

How Uploaded Data Is Used

1

Upload

The Administrator uploads sample complaint records through this page.

2

Store

Valid complaint records are made available to the system dataset.

3

Combine

Sample records can be combined with complaint records submitted by residents.

4

Split

The complaint dataset is divided into training and testing data before model building.

5

Train

The Random Forest model is trained using the available training records.

6

Evaluate

The model is evaluated using the testing records before prediction results are stored.
