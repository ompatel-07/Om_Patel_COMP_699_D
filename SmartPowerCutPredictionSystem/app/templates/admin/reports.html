System Workflow

Data Flow

Resident complaints are stored in the system and can contribute to the complaint dataset. Administrator uploaded sample records can also contribute to the dataset used by the prediction workflow.

The prediction workflow prepares training and testing records, evaluates the Random Forest model, generates prediction results, and stores the results for future analysis.
