Role-Based Access

Staff Responsibilities

🧑‍💼

Electricity Department Staff

Views assigned complaints, accepts complaints for processing, updates complaint status, adds repair notes, resolves complaints, and reviews complaint history for a specific location.

🧑‍⚖️

Senior Electricity Officer

Reviews escalated complaints, assigns escalated complaints to appropriate staff, and records the final action taken.

Authorization Workflow

Staff Registration Process

1

Registration Request

The authorized user registers by selecting the required department role.

2

Administrator Review

The registration remains restricted until the Administrator reviews the request.

3

Approval

The Administrator approves the account when the registration is authorized.

4

Dashboard Access

After approval and activation, the user can sign in and access the appropriate role dashboard.
